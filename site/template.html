01 · The math

What $50,000 takes

Where you're starting from: about 100 TikTok followers. At that size, $50,000 this month isn't a realistic target. Reviewers who publish their Benable income report about $80 to a few thousand dollars a month, and a realistic first month for a 100-follower account is roughly $0 to a few hundred dollars.

Why it can still grow fast. On TikTok, reach comes from the For You page, not your follower count, so one video that takes off can bring more views in a day than your account has had all year. Pinterest works the same way: pins rank by how well they match searches. That's why this plan is built around lots of videos and lots of pins.

When the money comes. Black Friday (Nov 27) through mid-December is when gift lists earn the most. October is for building the audience and pins that pay off then. Sales also stay pending until the retailer's return window closes, so October earnings usually become payable later in the quarter.

What moves the number. How much you earn per order. A $5 commission needs about three times the traffic of a $15 one, so these lists lean on high-ticket items and on retailers that pay more than Amazon.

04 · The lists

Eight lists, ready to paste

In Benable, start a new list and paste the title and description. For each item, tap Find it, copy the product link, and add it to the list. Benable pulls in the photo and price. Then paste the blurb and rewrite it in your own words. Delete anything you wouldn't recommend to a friend. Price bands: $ under $50 · $$ $50–150 · $$$ $150–400 · $$$$ $400+.

05 · Rules

Keep every dollar you earn

Do

  • Only list things you'd recommend to a friend. Rewrite each blurb in your own voice, and add your own experience where you have it.
  • Disclose affiliate links in your bio, list descriptions, pins and videos (#affiliate or "I earn from links"). The FTC requires it.
  • When a product is sold in several places, link the retailer that pays the best rate.
  • Use titles people actually search for on Pinterest and Google, like "gifts for her 2026".
  • Connect your Amazon Associates account. Amazon also closes accounts that don't make 3 sales in their first 180 days.

Don't

  • Claim you've used something you haven't.
  • Buy clicks, use bots or buy through your own links. Retailers void those commissions and can close the account.
  • Put Amazon affiliate links directly in emails or PDFs. Amazon doesn't allow it, so link to your Benable list instead.
  • Quote Amazon prices in posts. They change constantly. Say "on sale" and send people to the list.
  • Buy followers or views. Fake engagement tanks your reach and can get the account removed.
  • Pay for ads before testing small. At 3–6% commission, most paid traffic loses money.
