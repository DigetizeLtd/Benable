# October 2026 plan: the road to $50K in Benable commissions

## The math

Commissions = list views × click rate × purchase rate × order value × commission rate.

| Strategy | Rate | Avg order | Click → order | Commission / order | Views needed for $50K | Per day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mostly Amazon | 3% | $60 | 5% | $1.80 | 2,222,222 | ~71,700 |
| Balanced mix | 6% | $90 | 4% | $5.40 | 925,926 | ~29,900 |
| Premium picks | 8% | $180 | 2.5% | $14.40 | 555,556 | ~17,900 |

All three assume 25% of list viewers click a product. These are planning assumptions, not measured Benable numbers. Swap in your own from the Benable dashboard after the first week.

**Where you're starting from:** reviewers who publish their Benable income report roughly $80 to a few thousand dollars a month. $50K is far beyond that. It needs a large audience that already trusts your picks, or videos that take off during the Prime and Black Friday windows.

**The lever:** commission per order. High-ticket items (Airwrap, Dyson, espresso machines, mattresses) and retailers that pay more than Amazon (Sephora, Ulta, brand sites) cut the traffic you need by more than half.

**Timing:** sales stay *pending* until the retailer's return window closes, so money you earn in October usually becomes payable later in Q4.

## Day by day

**Thu Oct 1: Set up**
- [ ] Connect your Amazon Associates account in Benable, or apply today. Without it, Amazon items earn nothing.
- [ ] Profile: a real photo, a one-line bio about what you recommend, and an affiliate disclosure.
- [ ] Send your Benable referral link to creator friends. Reviews report +10% at 3 referrals and +20% at 10, so confirm in the app.

**Fri Oct 2 – Sat Oct 3: Build the Prime list**
- [ ] Build `lists/01-prime-big-deal-days.md` in Benable. Rewrite each blurb in your voice and cut anything you wouldn't stand behind.
- [ ] 5 Pinterest pins (tall 2:3, search-style titles like "Prime Day deals worth buying 2026").
- [ ] 3 short videos: "Prime Day is Tuesday. The only 5 things I'd buy."

**Sun Oct 4 – Mon Oct 5: Launch the gift guides**
- [ ] Publish Holiday Gift Guide, Gifts for Her and Gifts for Him, and link them to each other.
- [ ] Countdown post for the Prime list. Email your list if you have one, linking to the Benable list rather than to Amazon.
- [ ] 5 pins each for the three gift guides.

**Tue Oct 6 – Wed Oct 7: Prime Big Deal Days** (deal drops at 12:01am, 8am and 1pm PT)
- [ ] Refresh the Prime list after each drop and move the deepest discounts to the top.
- [ ] A story or short video after every drop, with the list link.
- [ ] A last-hours push Wednesday evening.

**Thu Oct 8 – Sun Oct 18: Gift guide push**
- [ ] Publish Kids & Teens, Gifts Under $50 and Home Upgrades.
- [ ] Post one recipient guide every day or two, each linking to its list.
- [ ] Pin 3–5 new pins a day.
- [ ] Answer gift questions in communities that allow links. Help first; spam gets you banned.

**Mon Oct 19 – Sat Oct 31: Double down**
- [ ] Check the Benable dashboard. Make more content about the lists and items that sell, and move top sellers up.
- [ ] Replace items that get clicks but no sales.
- [ ] Prep November: Black Friday is Nov 27 and Cyber Monday is Nov 30.

## Rules

**Do:** only list things you'd recommend to a friend · disclose affiliate links (FTC) · link the retailer that pays best · use searchable titles · keep your Amazon account active (3 sales in the first 180 days).

**Don't:** claim you've used something you haven't · buy clicks, use bots or buy through your own links · put Amazon affiliate links directly in emails or PDFs · quote Amazon prices in posts · pay for ads before testing small.
