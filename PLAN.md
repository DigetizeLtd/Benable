# October 2026 plan: starting from 100 TikTok followers

## Where you're starting

At about 100 followers, $50,000 this month isn't a realistic target. Reviewers who publish their Benable income report about $80 to a few thousand dollars a month, and a realistic first month for a 100-follower account is roughly $0 to a few hundred dollars.

It can still grow fast. TikTok reach comes from the For You page, not follower count, so one video that takes off can outdo months of posting. Pinterest ranks pins by how well they match searches, not by followers. The plan below is built around volume on both.

Black Friday (Nov 27) through mid-December is when gift lists earn the most. October is for building the audience and pins that pay off then.

## October goals

- **Hit the $80 payout.** About 1,500 list views at the balanced mix below.
- **Reach 1,000 TikTok followers.** About 30 a day from here.
- **Have 150+ pins live by Oct 31**, so Pinterest traffic is building before Black Friday.

## The math

Commissions = list views × click rate × purchase rate × order value × commission rate.

| Strategy | Rate | Avg order | Click → order | Commission / order | Views needed for $50K | Views needed for $80 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mostly Amazon | 3% | $60 | 5% | $1.80 | 2,222,222 | 3,556 |
| Balanced mix | 6% | $90 | 4% | $5.40 | 925,926 | 1,481 |
| Premium picks | 8% | $180 | 2.5% | $14.40 | 555,556 | 889 |

All three assume 25% of list viewers click a product. These are planning assumptions, not measured Benable numbers. Swap in your own from the Benable dashboard after the first week.

Sales stay *pending* until the retailer's return window closes, so money earned in October usually becomes payable later in Q4.

## Day by day

**Thu Oct 1: Set up**
- [ ] Switch TikTok to a Business account (Settings → Account → Switch to Business Account). It's free and reversible, and it unlocks the bio link now instead of at 1,000 followers. The trade-off is that you only get the commercial music library.
- [ ] Put your Benable profile or the Holiday Gift Guide in your TikTok bio link, with "I earn from links" in the bio.
- [ ] Connect your Amazon Associates account in Benable, or apply today. Without it, Amazon items earn nothing.
- [ ] Set up a Pinterest business account with boards named like searches: "Prime Day Deals 2026", "Gifts for Her 2026" and so on.

**Fri Oct 2 – Mon Oct 5: Three lists, not seven**
- [ ] Publish only three lists for now: Prime Big Deal Days, the Holiday Gift Guide, and the one recipient list you know best.
- [ ] Post 2–3 TikToks a day from the Prime scripts on the page. The first two seconds decide the For You page.
- [ ] Make 5–10 pins a day linking straight to your lists (tall 2:3 images, search-style titles).

**Tue Oct 6 – Wed Oct 7: Prime Big Deal Days** (deal drops at 12:01am, 8am and 1pm PT)
- [ ] Post a short video right after each drop with the three best deals and "full list in my bio".
- [ ] Refresh the Prime list after each drop and move the best discounts to the top.
- [ ] Reply to every comment, and answer "link?" comments with a video reply.

**Thu Oct 8 – Sun Oct 18: Find what works**
- [ ] Keep posting 2–3 TikToks a day from the gift-guide scripts, with a different hook each time.
- [ ] When a video gets 5× your usual views, post two more like it within 48 hours.
- [ ] Publish the other lists once you're getting a steady 30+ list visits a day.
- [ ] Keep pinning 5–10 pins a day.

**Mon Oct 19 – Sat Oct 31: Build for November**
- [ ] Move the most-clicked items to the top of each list and swap out items nobody clicks.
- [ ] Turn your best video into a series.
- [ ] Refresh pins and titles for Black Friday (Nov 27) and Cyber Monday (Nov 30).

The ten video scripts (hook, what to show, caption) are on the page in `site/index.html`.

## Rules

**Do:** only list things you'd recommend to a friend · disclose affiliate links in bio, lists, pins and videos (FTC) · link the retailer that pays best · use searchable titles · keep your Amazon account active (3 sales in the first 180 days).

**Don't:** claim you've used something you haven't · buy followers, views or clicks · buy through your own links · put Amazon affiliate links directly in emails or PDFs · quote Amazon prices in posts · pay for ads before testing small.
